# Toronto farmers’ markets for vegan shoppers

A vegan who cooks should use [Brick Works](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/evergreen-brick-works-saturday-farmers-market) or [The Stop](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-stops-farmers-market) for their fresh produce on Saturday, and should stop by [Earth & City](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/earth-city) and [Live on Chocolate](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/live-on-chocolate) for delectable vegan snacks.

---

## Saturday: Brick Works

[Evergreen Brick Works Saturday Farmers Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/evergreen-brick-works-saturday-farmers-market) · 550 Bayview Avenue · **8 AM–1 PM** · year-round

As of September 27, 2026, [Evergreen Brick Works](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/evergreen-brick-works-saturday-farmers-market) runs Saturdays, 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., and the local food court stays open until 2:00 p.m.

The Saturday market connects shoppers with Ontario farmers and producers, you can be ensured you're supporting local too with Evergreen claiming that the vendors are within 205 km. of The Brick Works. Although you won't find many vegan snacks, our vegan cooks will find all types of farmers and producers for those seasonal greens.

The market is at 550 Bayview Avenue, outdoors under the pavilions from May through October and indoors in the TD Future Cities Centre from November through April. Late September is inside the outdoor season.

---

## Saturday: The Stop

[The Stop’s Farmers’ Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-stops-farmers-market) · 601 Christie Street · **8 AM–1 PM** · year-round

As of September 27, 2026, [The Stop](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-stops-farmers-market) runs its market at Wychwood Barns, 601 Christie Street, Saturdays, 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. It is outdoors from May through October, indoors from November through April, and closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day. The Stop sells produce, alongside meat, dairy, and prepared food. So again if you're looking for the finest local produce, you're in luck, but it'll be a challenge to find much else within the vegan vein.

From November through April, both markets move indoors, so a kitchen-minded vegan can keep the Saturday trip past October.

---

## Thursday: Dufferin Grove

[Dufferin Grove Organic Farmers’ Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/dufferin-grove-organic-farmers-market) · 875 Dufferin Street · **3 PM–7 PM** · year-round

As of September 27, 2026, [Dufferin Grove Organic Farmers' Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/dufferin-grove-organic-farmers-market) runs every Thursday, 3:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., at 875 Dufferin Street. The market says the park has had a year-round market since November 2002. It also says it keeps more than half the vendor list as farms, and that vendors must grow, raise, or make what they sell so you can be sure you're getting that Toronto-fresh guarantee everytime.

Dufferin Grove describes vegan fare at [Earth & City](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/earth-city): coconut macaroons, cowboy cookies, and juice-pulp crackers, in person every Thursday from 3:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. If you inquire, I'm sure they'll be able to point you to even more vegan menu options.

**Vendors to look for:**

- [Earth & City](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/earth-city) · 3 PM–7 PM

---

## Saturday: Withrow Park

[Withrow Park Farmers’ Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/withrow-park-farmers-market) · 725 Logan Avenue · **9 AM–1 PM** · May 30–Oct 17

[Withrow Park Farmers' Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/withrow-park-farmers-market) is in the northeast corner of Withrow Park, where Carlaw Avenue meets McConnell Avenue. As of September 27, 2026, it runs Saturdays, 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., from May 30 through October 17.

Withrow lists [Live On Chocolate](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/live-on-chocolate) and several businesses whose names include "farm," and it does not say which of them grow the food. [Live On Chocolate](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/live-on-chocolate) is described as making vegan, dairy-free desserts, listed as full time, with a note to check who is there each week.

**Vendors to look for:**

- [Live On Chocolate](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/live-on-chocolate) · 9 AM–1 PM

The macaroons, cookies, crackers, and desserts are snacks. You can't necessarily make preemptive lunch plans, but the vegans snack you'll encounter are to die for and we have a hunch you might just ruin your appetite.
