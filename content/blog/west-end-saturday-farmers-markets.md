# West-end Saturday farmers’ markets: Junction, The Stop, Brick Works

Here’s our take on the best farmers' markets to visit on Saturday mornings on the west and midtown side of town: Bayview ([Evergreen Brick Works Saturday Farmers Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/evergreen-brick-works-saturday-farmers-market)), Wychwood ([The Stop’s Farmers’ Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-stops-farmers-market)), and Keele ([The Junction Farmers Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-junction-farmers-market)). Get all the hours, a peek at their vendors, and whatever else we could find all in one place.

---

## Saturday, early: Evergreen Brick Works

[Evergreen Brick Works Saturday Farmers Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/evergreen-brick-works-saturday-farmers-market) · 550 Bayview Avenue · **8 AM–1 PM**

Toronto’s biggest year-round Saturday market. Outdoors under the pavilions **May 2–Oct 31**; indoors in the TD Future Cities Centre **Nov 1–Apr 30**.

**Vendors to look for:**

- [Joyfully Organic](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/joyfully-organic-farm) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [Cutting Veg Toronto](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/the-cutting-veg) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [Fresh and Tasty Mushrooms](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/fresh-and-tasty-mushrooms) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [Monforte Cheese](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/monforte-dairy) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [St. John’s Bakery](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/st-john-s-bakery) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [GoodLot](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/goodlot-farmstead-brewing-company) · 8 AM–1 PM

## Saturday, mid-morning: The Stop at Wychwood Barns

[The Stop’s Farmers’ Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-stops-farmers-market) · 601 Christie Street · **8 AM–1 PM** · year-round

Rain or shine at Wychwood Barns. This is the Saturday farmers’ market — **not** The Stop’s Tuesday Good Food Market on Davenport.

**Vendors to look for:**

- [Cutting Veg Toronto](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/the-cutting-veg) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [Clover Roads Organic](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/clover-roads-organic-farm) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [Forbes Wild Foods](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/forbes-wild-foods) · 8 AM–1 PM
- [Nith Valley Organics](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/nith-valley-organics) · 8 AM–1 PM

## Saturday, late morning: The Junction

[The Junction Farmers Market](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-junction-farmers-market) · 275 Keele Street · **9 AM–1 PM** · May 16–Oct 31

A west-end outdoor Saturday. If you did Brick Works or Wychwood first, it'd be better to start here later.

**Vendors to look for:**

- [Bizjak Farms](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/bizjak-farms) · 9 AM–1 PM
- [County Left Farm](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/county-left-farm) · 9 AM–1 PM
- [Kooner Farm Organics](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/kooner-farms) · 9 AM–1 PM
- [It’s Giving Farm](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/it-s-giving-farm) · 9 AM–1 PM
- [Fifth Town](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/fifth-town-artisan-cheese) · 9 AM–1 PM
- [Blue Collar Bakery](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/blue-collar-bakery) · 9 AM–1 PM

## MarketRegular Recommended Saturday Guide:

- **8 AM:** [Brick Works](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/evergreen-brick-works-saturday-farmers-market) (Bayview) or [The Stop](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-stops-farmers-market) (Christie), both of which are open early.
- **9 AM:** [Junction](https://www.marketregular.com/markets/the-junction-farmers-market) (Keele) if you want to finish the circuit outdoors, and choose from their collection of 50+ vendors.
- **MarketRegular Tip:** [Cutting Veg](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/the-cutting-veg) and [Monforte](https://www.marketregular.com/vendors/monforte-dairy) sell at both 8 AM markets, so pick Brick Works *or* The Stop, then finish at the Junction after 9. But remember that the Junction is only in season for about one more full month, so try to plan a visit in the coming weeks.
