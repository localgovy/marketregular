# MarketRegular Originality Policy

Version: 0.1

## Principle

Outside publications teach craft.

They do not provide prose templates.

## The writer may learn abstract techniques such as:

- article structure
- information sequencing
- level of specificity
- produce vocabulary
- paragraph function
- sentence-length variation
- use of comparison
- degree of restraint

## The writer may not imitate:

- distinctive phrases
- distinctive metaphors
- recognizable sentence sequences
- unique paragraph structures
- writer-specific verbal habits
- a named writer's voice

## Reference separation

The final Writer should normally receive MarketRegular's distilled
editorial rules and approved MarketRegular examples.

The Writer should not receive a large bundle of raw outside articles.

## Similarity

Mechanical similarity flags are editorial review triggers.

They are not legal determinations.

High similarity requires human inspection.

## Goal

MarketRegular should become increasingly self-referential in a good way:

outside journalism
→ abstract lessons
→ MarketRegular rules
→ MarketRegular articles
→ MarketRegular examples
→ future MarketRegular articles
