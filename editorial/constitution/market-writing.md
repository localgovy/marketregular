# MarketRegular Market Writing

Version: 0.1

## A market guide must answer practical questions

Why this market?

What is it particularly good for?

When should someone go?

What kind of shopping trip does it suit?

What distinguishes it from another market?

Which claims are current and which are stable?

## Avoid generic market language

Do not automatically describe markets as:

bustling
vibrant
charming
lively
community hubs
hidden gems
foodie destinations

These descriptions require evidence and often communicate very little.

## Compare function, not hype

Weak:

"This is one of Toronto's best markets."

Better:

"This is particularly useful when you want serious produce shopping
and several prepared-food options in the same trip."

## Vendor rosters

Never imply that a vendor will be present on a specific date unless
the evidence supports it.

## Atmosphere

Do not invent atmosphere from a website listing.

A market having many vendors does not prove:

- crowds
- noise
- smells
- friendliness
- excitement
- queue lengths
