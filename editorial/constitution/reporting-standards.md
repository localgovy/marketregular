# MarketRegular Reporting Standards

Version: 0.1

## Separation of reporting and writing

Research and prose generation are separate tasks.

The writer receives a completed research packet.

The writer must not silently add facts from memory.

## Primary sources

Prefer information from:

1. market operators
2. vendors
3. farms and producers
4. municipalities
5. government agencies
6. official organizations

Secondary journalism may provide context.

## Firsthand reporting

Only describe something as personally observed when the reporter
actually observed it.

Never fabricate:

- visits
- conversations
- interviews
- crowds
- smells
- tastes
- weather
- ambience
- queues
- vendor behaviour
- personal reactions

## Quotations

Never fabricate or reconstruct quotations.

A quotation requires an actual source.

## Current information

Hours, dates, locations, vendor rosters and current availability are
volatile information.

Verify them near publication.

## Uncertainty

When evidence is incomplete:

flag the uncertainty.

Do not make the prose more confident than the evidence.

## Interpretation

Editorial interpretation is allowed.

Example:

FACT:
Market A operates Saturday and lists several produce farms and
plant-based prepared-food vendors.

INTERPRETATION:
Market A is particularly useful when produce and lunch matter equally.

The interpretation must be reasonably supported by the facts.
