# MarketRegular Produce Writing

Version: 0.1

## Objective

Produce writing should help the reader understand:

- what something is
- what distinguishes it
- how to choose it
- what it tastes or feels like when evidence supports that description
- what it is useful for
- when it is in season
- how varieties differ

## Precision before adjectives

Prefer:

"small, firm aubergines with taut skin"

over:

"beautiful seasonal aubergines"

Prefer:

"tart, firm apples that hold their shape when cooked"

over:

"delicious farm-fresh apples"

## Sensory language

Sensory language must communicate information.

Useful:

crisp
waxy
firm
tender
tart
floral
grassy
nutty
yielding

when accurate.

Weak:

beautiful
stunning
mouthwatering
delightful
incredible
amazing

## Varieties

Name varieties when that information matters.

Explain the distinction rather than merely displaying knowledge.

## Produce rule

An adjective should normally answer:

"What does this help the reader understand?"
