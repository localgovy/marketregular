# MarketRegular Fact Policy

Version: 0.1

## Fundamental rule

Every externally verifiable factual assertion in a draft must be
traceable to the article research packet.

## Claim IDs

Research claims use:

MR-001
MR-002
MR-003

Interpretations use:

MRI-001
MRI-002

## Draft markers

Internal drafts preserve markers:

<!-- claim: MR-001 -->

or:

<!-- claim: MR-001 MR-002 -->

These comments are removed only after verification.

## Status values

verified
unverified
conflict
stale

Only verified claims may normally be published as facts.

## Confidence

high
medium
low

Confidence and verification are different concepts.

A claim may have a source but still have low confidence.

## Volatile facts

The following require freshness checks:

- market hours
- operating dates
- current location
- vendor roster
- current menu
- seasonal opening/closing
- event dates
- temporary closures

## Starting freshness policy

open/closed today: 1 day
weekly hours: 3 days
seasonal start/end: 7 days
vendor roster: 7 days
dietary positioning: 30 days
address: 30 days
background/history: 180 days

These are MarketRegular editorial defaults, not universal truths.

## Conflicting evidence

Never silently choose one source.

Record the conflict.

Resolve it before publication or write around the uncertainty.
